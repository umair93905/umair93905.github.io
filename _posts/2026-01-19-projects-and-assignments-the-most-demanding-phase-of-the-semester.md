As the second semester progressed, the academic workload increased significantly. Lectures, laboratory tasks, quizzes, and examinations were already demanding, but the introduction of multiple projects and assignments made this period even more challenging. Every course seemed to have its own practical component, and students were expected to apply the concepts they had learned throughout the semester.

This phase was one of the busiest periods of my university journey because several important projects had to be completed simultaneously. Managing deadlines, understanding project requirements, and maintaining progress in regular coursework required careful planning and dedication. Although the workload was demanding, it provided valuable opportunities to strengthen both technical and problem-solving skills.

## The Beginning of Project Season

The project season began when instructors started assigning practical tasks designed to test our understanding of course concepts. Unlike regular assignments, these projects required long-term effort and deeper understanding. Students had to plan their work carefully because completing a project involved multiple stages, including research, design, implementation, testing, and documentation.

At first, balancing project work with regular academic responsibilities seemed difficult. Every subject demanded attention, and each project came with its own objectives and challenges. There were times when multiple deadlines approached simultaneously, creating pressure and requiring effective time management.

Despite these difficulties, I understood that projects were among the most important learning experiences of the semester. They provided opportunities to apply classroom knowledge to practical situations and develop skills that could not be learned through theory alone.

## Working on OOP and DLD Projects

One of the major projects during this period was related to Object-Oriented Programming. The project required us to apply OOP concepts in a practical application and demonstrate our understanding of programming principles. Through this project, I gained experience in software design, coding, debugging, and problem-solving.

At the same time, we were also working on a project for Digital Logic Design (DLD). This project focused on implementing digital systems and applying the concepts learned in class. It required careful analysis, logical thinking, and attention to detail. Working on both projects simultaneously was challenging because each required a different technical approach.

The OOP project strengthened my programming skills, while the DLD project improved my understanding of digital systems and logical design. Together, they provided valuable practical experience and enhanced my confidence in handling technical tasks.

Managing these projects taught me the importance of planning and consistency. Small amounts of daily progress proved far more effective than attempting to complete large portions of work at the last moment.

## The Leukemia Prediction Database Project

Among all the projects completed during the semester, the Database Systems project was one of the most significant and memorable. Under the guidance of Dr. Bilal Ahmad, our group worked on a project titled "Leukemia Blood Cancer Survival Rate Prediction." The project focused on designing and managing a database system related to leukemia patient information and survival prediction analysis.

The project required us to apply database concepts learned throughout the semester, including database design, normalization, ER diagrams, relationships, and SQL implementation. Developing the project helped me understand how database systems can be used to organize and manage real-world information effectively.

Working on this project involved teamwork, research, and technical implementation. Every stage required careful planning and collaboration among group members. Through the project, I gained a deeper appreciation for the practical applications of database technology in healthcare and data management.

Dr. Bilal Ahmad provided valuable guidance throughout the project development process. His feedback and recommendations helped us improve the quality of our work and understand the importance of professional project development practices.

Looking back, this phase of the semester was demanding but highly educational. The combination of assignments and projects required significant effort, yet it also provided some of the most valuable learning experiences of my second semester. These projects strengthened my technical knowledge, improved my teamwork and problem-solving abilities, and prepared me for future academic and professional challenges.
