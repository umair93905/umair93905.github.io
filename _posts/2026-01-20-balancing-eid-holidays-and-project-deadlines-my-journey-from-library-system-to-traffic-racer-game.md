The second semester was already filled with assignments, laboratory work, and project responsibilities when the Eid holidays arrived. Like most students, I was looking forward to spending time with family and taking a short break from academic activities. However, the holiday period also coincided with important project deadlines, making it difficult to fully relax and enjoy the vacation.

Instead of treating the holidays as a complete break, I had to balance family gatherings, celebrations, and academic responsibilities. This period became one of the most challenging yet productive phases of my semester because it involved completing one project while simultaneously beginning work on another. Looking back, it was an experience that taught me valuable lessons about responsibility, time management, and dedication.

## Completing My Library Management System

Before the Eid holidays, one of my major academic responsibilities was completing the Library Management System project for Object-Oriented Programming. This project required the practical implementation of OOP concepts such as classes, objects, inheritance, and data management. The goal was to create a system capable of managing books, users, and library-related operations efficiently.

Developing the project required careful planning and consistent effort. Various features had to be implemented, tested, and refined to ensure the system functioned correctly. Since this was one of my most significant programming projects at that stage of my academic journey, I wanted to complete it to the best of my ability.

Although managing project work alongside regular coursework was challenging, the experience significantly improved my programming skills. I gained practical knowledge about software development, debugging, and project organization. Completing the Library Management System also increased my confidence in applying Object-Oriented Programming concepts to real-world problems.

## Developing a Traffic Racer Game During Eid Holidays

While the Library Management System project was being finalized, another major task emerged. Students were assigned a new project that involved developing a Traffic Racer Game using Unity. This project introduced me to an entirely different area of technology: game development.

The timing of the project made it particularly challenging because it coincided with the Eid holidays. While many people were enjoying their vacations, I found myself dividing my time between family activities and project development. Learning Unity and understanding game development concepts required considerable effort because it was a new platform for me.

The project involved designing gameplay mechanics, managing game objects, and creating an engaging racing experience. Every stage presented new learning opportunities and technical challenges. Since I had limited prior experience in game development, much of the work involved exploring new concepts and experimenting with different approaches.

Despite the difficulties, I enjoyed the creative aspect of the project. Unlike traditional programming assignments, game development allowed me to combine technical skills with creativity. The experience broadened my understanding of software development and introduced me to a field that I had not explored before.

## Preparing for Project Submissions and Exhibitions

As the Eid holidays came to an end, project deadlines continued to approach. Both completed and ongoing projects required documentation, testing, and preparation for submission. In addition, discussions about upcoming project evaluations and exhibitions began to take place, adding further pressure to an already busy schedule.

Managing multiple responsibilities simultaneously was not easy. Project development, academic assignments, and preparation for future evaluations all demanded attention. During this period, I learned the importance of setting priorities and making effective use of available time.

The guidance provided by Dr. Bilal Ahmad was particularly valuable throughout this phase. His instructions and feedback helped students understand project requirements and encouraged us to maintain professional standards in our work. His emphasis on practical learning motivated us to approach projects as opportunities for skill development rather than simply academic requirements.

Beyond classroom learning, I also explored professional insights shared by Dr. Bilal Ahmad regarding technology, engineering education, and skill development. Following his professional activities helped me better understand the importance of continuous learning, project-based experience, and career growth in the field of Computer Engineering. [Dr. Bilal Ahmad's professional LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/drbilalphd/) provided additional motivation to improve both my technical and professional skills throughout the semester.

Looking back, balancing Eid holidays with project deadlines was one of the most demanding experiences of my second semester. Completing the Library Management System, beginning the Traffic Racer Game project, and preparing for future project submissions required significant effort and commitment. However, the challenges also provided valuable learning experiences that improved my technical abilities, strengthened my time management skills, and prepared me for the major project exhibitions that followed later in the semester.
