Along with OOP, another important subject in my second semester was Database Management. This subject introduced me to the world of data handling and management, which is a key part of modern software systems. In Database, we started by learning about different types of keys, such as primary keys and foreign keys, and their importance in maintaining data integrity. After that, we learned how to create tables and perform different operations like SELECT, INSERT, and DELETE. One of the most interesting parts was learning how to join tables, which allowed us to work with data from multiple sources.

This helped me understand how real-world databases function. We also learned how to create complete databases and manage them effectively. In addition, we started learning how to upload our work on GitHub, which is an essential skill for developers. This gave me exposure to version control and project management. At the same time, OOP concepts continued to strengthen my programming skills.

Combining OOP with Database knowledge gave me a better understanding of how software applications are built and managed. Overall, this semester helped me develop both programming and data management skills, which are essential for my future career. Our teacher Dr. Bilal explained database concepts in a practical way and encouraged us to practice regularly. This experience helped me grow academically and personally.

I learned the importance of consistency, discipline, communication, and continuous learning. Every challenge taught me a new lesson and every success increased my confidence. I discussed ideas with classmates, sought guidance from teachers, and spent extra time improving my understanding. The journey was not always easy, but it strengthened my determination and encouraged me to keep moving forward. I believe these experiences will remain valuable throughout my university life and future professional career.
