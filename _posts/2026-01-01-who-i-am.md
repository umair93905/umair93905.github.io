My name is Muhammad Umair, and I am currently a second-semester student of Computer Engineering at UET Faisalabad. I belong to Jura Sakesar, District and Tehsil Sargodha. My academic journey reflects a gradual transformation, where I explored different fields before finding my interest in technology and engineering. I completed my matriculation from Dar-e-Arqam Model High School Bhalwal with 871 marks out of 1100 in Biology. At that stage, my academic direction was inclined towards medical sciences.

However, as I progressed further, I started thinking more deeply about my future and career goals. I realized that my interests were shifting towards technology and problem-solving. Keeping this in mind, I made an important decision at the college level to switch my field from Biology to Computer Science. I enrolled in Punjab College Bhalwal in ICS (Physics). This decision played a crucial role in shaping my future.

During my intermediate studies, I worked hard and achieved 899 marks out of 1200. This gave me confidence to pursue a technical field in higher education. After completing my intermediate education, I entered a phase of uncertainty where I had to decide my career path. Initially, I did not have a clear plan, but I was determined to explore opportunities. Over time, my interest in engineering and computing grew stronger, which eventually led me to pursue Computer Engineering.

Currently, I am studying at UET Faisalabad and have completed my first semester with a GPA of 2.94. Although it is not perfect, it reflects my transition from college to university and the challenges I faced along the way. I am continuously working on improving my academic performance and technical skills. Overall, my journey has taught me the importance of adaptability, hard work, and self-belief. I am motivated to keep learning and growing in the field of Computer Engineering.

This experience helped me grow academically and personally. I learned the importance of consistency, discipline, communication, and continuous learning. Every challenge taught me a new lesson and every success increased my confidence. I discussed ideas with classmates, sought guidance from teachers, and spent extra time improving my understanding. The journey was not always easy, but it strengthened my determination and encouraged me to keep moving forward.

I believe these experiences will remain valuable throughout my university life and future professional career. They improved my problem-solving abilities, time management, adaptability, and willingness to learn new things.
