After the final exams, the next phase was waiting for the result. I was very anxious because I knew that my performance in the midterms, especially in Discrete Mathematics, had not been good. Even though I had improved in the finals, I was still worried about the overall outcome. When the result was announced, I found that I had received a C+ grade in Discrete Mathematics, and my overall GPA was 2.94. At first, I felt disappointed because I had hoped for a better GPA.

For a moment, I even felt like giving up, thinking that my efforts were not enough. However, instead of staying negative, I decided to talk to my friends and teachers. I shared my concerns with them, and they encouraged me a lot. My teachers told me that 2.94 is not a bad GPA and that I still have time to improve in future semesters. Their support helped me regain my confidence.

I realized that one semester does not define my entire future. What matters is how I learn from my mistakes and improve myself. In the end, I accepted my result and decided to move forward with a positive mindset. This experience taught me the importance of patience, resilience, and self-belief. It also motivated me to work harder in the next semester.

This experience helped me grow academically and personally. I learned the importance of consistency, discipline, communication, and continuous learning. Every challenge taught me a new lesson and every success increased my confidence. I discussed ideas with classmates, sought guidance from teachers, and spent extra time improving my understanding. The journey was not always easy, but it strengthened my determination and encouraged me to keep moving forward.

I believe these experiences will remain valuable throughout my university life and future professional career. They improved my problem-solving abilities, time management, adaptability, and willingness to learn new things.
